## The workspace canvas

**Projects** opens on the workspace canvas: one board with every project in the workspace, its experiments and runs inside it, and the recorded links between them drawn as arrows. Choose **All workspaces** in the workspace menu for one combined board.

- **Move around.** Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, and use **−**, **+** and **Fit** at the bottom right. **Cmd**/**Ctrl**+drag rearranges projects and experiments; hold **Alt**/**Option** to skip snapping. Positions and the camera are saved in your browser, per workspace.
- **Zoom changes the drawing.** From 45% up, project frames show their experiments and runs, plus a collapsed **Research papers** list where the project has papers. Between 20% and 45%, projects become titles grouped by meaning on coloured backgrounds; click a group to filter the board to it. At 20% and below, each group becomes one label with its project and run counts.
- **Filter by time.** **Live**, **24h**, **7d**, **30d** or **All** (the default), at the bottom left.
- **Read a link.** Hover an arrow for its **Link** popup: the stored reason, when its writer gave one, and one word for where the link came from — **Fork**, **Agent**, **SDK**, **Person**, **Backfill** or **Session**. Click the arrow to pin it.
- **Open anything.** Click a project, experiment or run to open its page. Hover a project or run for a preview.

The Search / Ask bar stays open at the bottom of the canvas, and **Cmd**/**Ctrl**+**K** focuses it. A search highlights its matches on the board and keeps your zoom. The **Workspace details** pane beside the board holds the workspace overview and a project directory, newest first.

The canvas is on for everyone. To go back to the Projects list, turn off **Workspace canvas** in **Settings** → **Preferences**. With the canvas on, a project's own page also has an **Overview** / **Canvas** toggle: the canvas shows its experiments and runs, or its papers on a `research` project.

## Things worth knowing up front

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The workspace switcher scopes almost everything" icon="layer-group">
    Projects, the canvas and Search are all filtered by the workspace you are in. **Choose All workspaces for cross-workspace discovery** — a project you cannot find is usually a project filed somewhere else.

    Your selection persists. A missing selection falls back to the oldest workspace.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Summaries are written by the recorders" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    A project's or experiment's **Summary** is written by the recorders — the [Probe daemon](/agents/daemon)'s writer, or an agent working inline — as work happens. There is no regenerate button, and nothing is queued: an entity nobody has written up yet says "No summary yet." The page history labels each write by who made it and from where (`via daemon`, `via cli`).

    See [summaries](/tracking/summaries).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Computed is never shown as captured" icon="calculator">
    Expression panels carry an **ƒ** chip; derived series carry a **derived** chip with producer and computed-at on hover. Grouping, smoothing and pivoting are never presented as source rows, and a panel's value table is labeled computed.

    This is a hard rule throughout the dashboard: post-hoc numbers do not masquerade as training-loop capture.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reads are open, writes may not be" icon="lock">
    Every member of the team reads every workspace. What a workspace can restrict is who may **edit** what is filed in it. See [workspaces](/dashboard/workspaces).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An empty state is not an error state" icon="circle-exclamation">
    A failed load renders as a failure, never as "nothing here". "No papers recorded" on a project that has papers would be a confident lie about your own data, so the two states are kept distinct everywhere they appear.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Getting set up

**Install**, in the account menu, carries the one command you need and nothing else — everything that used to live in collapsed "if something is not working" sections now lives in [the wizard](/wizard/overview), which can act on the state instead of describing it.
